*Consultant Biologist and Project Lead · Ikama Peru Wildlife Rescue Center · Loreto, Peru · 2026–present*

Primates confiscated from the illegal pet and bushmeat trade arrive at rescue centres in large numbers, and few can be returned to the wild. I lead the assessment of whether an Amazonian site in Loreto can support the reintroduction of rescued woolly (*Lagothrix*) and howler (*Alouatta*) monkeys.

**What I do**

- Estimate abundance and density of resident primates through standardised line-transect surveys.
- Establish botanical plots to characterise vegetation composition, forest structure and food availability.
- Support the socio-ecological components: surveys of local residents and environmental education.
- Translate results into technical recommendations for the project and for Peruvian authorities.
