*Fellow, Primate Research Course · Cocha Cashu Biological Station — San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance Peru · Manu National Park · October–November 2024*

Lactation is the most energetically demanding stage for female primates. During the Cocha Cashu Primate Research Course I designed an independent pilot study comparing activity budgets and dietary preferences of lactating and non-lactating adult female Peruvian spider monkeys.

**What I did**

- Designed and applied an ethogram; completed 22 focal follows.
- Collected ecological data and non-invasive biological (faecal) samples.
- Analysed activity budgets and dietary overlap between reproductive states.

**Presented at:** Peruvian Primatological Congress, Lima (2026) — poster.
