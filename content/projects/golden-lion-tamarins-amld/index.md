*Volunteer · Associação Mico-Leão-Dourado (AMLD) · Rio de Janeiro, Brazil · January–February 2026*

The golden lion tamarin is one of the best-known conservation success stories among Neotropical primates, built on decades of reintroduction, habitat restoration and long-term monitoring in a fragmented Atlantic Forest landscape.

**What I did**

- Monitored reintroduced groups through radio-telemetry, direct observation and tracking of individually identified animals.
- Assisted with supervised capture, vaccination, morphometric measurements and health assessments.
- Supported agroforestry management, ecotourism and environmental education with school groups.
- Worked in a Portuguese-speaking multidisciplinary team.
